# First Day Entertainment

> First Day Entertainment Inc. is a Sunnyvale, California game studio building AI-powered games.
> Founded by Stephen Gou (formerly a founding engineer at Cohere) and Mike Mei, and funded by the
> a16z SPEEDRUN accelerator (batch 002) and MiraclePlus. The studio ships consumer game titles; it

## Products

- [Heroes of Magic & Cards](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3843360/Heroes_of_Magic__Cards/): A poker deckbuilding roguelike. Build a deck, draw a hand, and defeat enemies with pairs and straights in a fantasy world of monsters and magecraft. Distributed on Steam.
- [Mana Land](https://firstdaygg.itch.io/mana-land): A single-player RPG with multiplayer feel. Players adventure alongside conversational AI companions, with team-oriented play and community features and without the toxicity of online games. Published on itch.io.
